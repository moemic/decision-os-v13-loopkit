# Task-one New Repo Capsule Fit Audit (synthetic)

Destination / Purpose: a private, local workspace that drafts a volunteer
tool-return intake checklist from supplied facts. Work is text drafting and
handoff; no public surface, code, API, contact, audit, or automation is in
scope. Owner: Mira.

## Active guards and why

- Mira's Seat and local-only authority: avoids importing another owner's
  permissions. Source: hub `AGENTS.md` at `7a0ecdb`, and
  `docs/new_repo_scaffold_standard.md` at upstream `26c9eea` §Universal Core.
- Separate the three intake states; do not claim safety or availability from a
  ledger entry. Source: `source/intake_facts.md`; hub active evidence rule at
  `7a0ecdb:AGENTS.md`.
- Completion, UNKNOWN, and restart handoff: prevents an unfinished inspection
  from becoming an implicit next action. Source:
  `26c9eea:docs/new_repo_scaffold_standard.md` §§Universal Core/Handoff.

## Conditional guards and exact reconnect triggers

- If Mira requests publication or external contact, stop and obtain that
  separate authorization, then reassess public-surface and contact guards.
  Source: hub `7a0ecdb:AGENTS.md` and
  `26c9eea:templates/v13_build_capsule_minimum_contract.md` §Public Surface.
- If the purpose changes beyond private text drafting, re-run this audit
  before acting. Source: `26c9eea:docs/new_repo_scaffold_standard.md`
  §Fit Audit Re-evaluation Trigger.
- If Mira requests a handoff, read `docs/handoff_command.md`. Source:
  `26c9eea:docs/new_repo_scaffold_standard.md` §Handoff Command Requirement.

## Excluded guards and why

- Runtime/API, scraping, LLM evaluation, automation, money, and release guards:
  no such operation occurs in this text-only destination. Source:
  `26c9eea:templates/v13_build_capsule_minimum_contract.md`
  §Repo-specific Capsule Fit Audit; `README.md` purpose.
- The upstream author's current Gate, goals, active branch, and broad Field
  Note corpus: no destination need and no Mira authority comes from them.
  Source: hub `7a0ecdb:AGENTS.md`, `26c9eea:docs/fork_codex_quickstart.md`.

## UNKNOWN and recheck

- Actual inspection criteria and real workshop acceptance: not in
  `source/intake_facts.md`. The draft may say `inspection pending`, but cannot
  specify a safety check or claim it succeeded. Recheck when Mira supplies
  source facts before any operational use.

This is the sole capsule Fit Audit for this destination. It records the
selected payload; it does not authorize a new operation.
